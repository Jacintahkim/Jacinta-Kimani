Student & Financial Compliance Dashboards

ICT Business Intelligence Intern · Concordia College, Adelaide · Jun–Sep 2025

The problem

Concordia College's student and financial data lived across three disconnected systems — Synergetic (student information system), SEQTA (learning management system), and SQL Server — with no reliable way for leadership to see a unified, trustworthy picture of enrollment, academic progress, or compliance status.

What I did

  • Queried and joined records across all three systems using SQL (SSMS), auditing 1.5K+ student records for cross-system accuracy.
  • Identified and resolved 100+ data inconsistencies through ETL-style validation, so downstream reports could be trusted.
  • Built interactive Power BI dashboards surfacing enrollment trends and early-warning indicators for at-risk students.
  • Partnered directly with school leadership to design compliance dashboards that supported data-driven resource allocation decisions.

Outcome

The cleaned, unified data and dashboards gave leadership visibility they didn't have before, contributing to a 15% improvement in intervention efficiency — staff could identify and act on at-risk cases faster instead of manually cross-checking three systems.

Reporting Automation

Enrollment & Finance Report Automation

ICT Business Intelligence Intern · Concordia College, Adelaide · Jun–Sep 2025

The problem

Enrollment and finance staff were manually assembling recurring reports every month — a repetitive process that pulled time away from higher-value analysis and left room for manual error.

What I did

  • Automated 10+ recurring reports using Microsoft Report Builder (SSRS), replacing manual spreadsheet assembly with scheduled, standardized output.
  • Used SQL to structure the underlying queries feeding each report, ensuring consistency across enrollment and finance data.
  • Documented the reports so non-technical staff could run and interpret them without IT support.

Outcome

The automation freed up over 20 hours of manual work per month across enrollment and finance operations — time redirected toward analysis and decision-making instead of report assembly.

Process Mapping & Automation

Payment Services Automation Opportunity Log

ICT Intern · Opex Consulting PLT, Adelaide · Started Jun 2026 (in progress)

The problem

The Payment Services team's day-to-day workflows — spanning platforms like CommPay and Oreana PayWay — had never been formally mapped, making it hard to know where manual effort could be safely automated and where hard constraints (like items that must never be auto-resolved) needed to be respected.

What I did

  • Shadowed Payment Services Officers and ran structured workshops to observe and document current-state workflows in detail.
  • Identified automation candidates across multiple platforms, while flagging hard constraints and edge cases that rule out full automation.
  • Consolidated findings into a 12-item Automation Opportunity Log, prioritized by feasibility and impact.
  • Produced Confluence-ready process documentation so the wider team can act on the findings independently.

Outcome (in progress)

This work is feeding directly into the team's Phase 2 review, including a time-sensitive platform consolidation project. The opportunity log gives Opex a clear, prioritized roadmap for where automation will save the most time without compromising accuracy or compliance.
